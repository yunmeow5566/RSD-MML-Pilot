# Course Unit: Speech with Neural Networks

## Learning Goals
- Students can understand how audio is converted into high-dimensional vectors.
- Students can learn and implement classic algorithms like Hidden Markov Model and Vertibi.
- Students can use at least one open source solution to perform STT and TTS tasks. 

## Speech/Audio Problems
- Speech Recognition
- Speaker Identification
- Speaker Diarization
- Emotion Recognition from Speech
- Speech Segmentation
- Speech Synthesis (out of scope, can be added)
